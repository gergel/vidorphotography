About
2015
Vidor Gergely
Photographer & Videographer

I started photography as a child with my grandfather's Nikon D70. Over the years I switched to a Nikon D7200, and today I use 2× Canon C70 with RF lenses and DZO Cine optics for videography, and Canon R6II for photography. In my professional work, I work daily as a cinematographer, creating high-quality cinematic content for a wide range of clients and projects.

My experience spans a wide spectrum: school balls, concerts, after-parties, graduations, press conferences, weddings, and gastro photography for bars and restaurants.
